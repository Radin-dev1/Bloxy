Questions, answered without the sales pitch.

Is the workspace actually interactive?

Yes. It uses Three.js with orbit controls, selection, transforms, a real properties inspector, working tool panels, undo and redo, and live generated geometry.

What does Gemini do?

Gemini creates a Roblox action blueprint, then a second review pass checks scale, paths, collisions, structure, color, materials, and detail.

What does Tripo do?

Tripo handles text-to-3D and image-to-3D generation. Completed textured GLB models load into the viewport and can be downloaded.

Can Bloxy search real Roblox assets?

Yes. The Assets panel searches the official Creator Store and filters for verified creators.

Does generation modify my Studio project?

No. Pairing and approval are separate steps. Web generation does not automatically change your Roblox project.

How are Creator Store models handled?

You review the asset first. During Studio import, Bloxy removes embedded Lua source containers before placing the model.

Where are my API keys stored?

Gemini and Tripo keys are encrypted Cloudflare secrets. They are not shipped in GitHub Pages or exposed to Roblox Studio.

What can I edit?

Name, material, color, size, Anchored, and CanCollide are editable. Move, rotate, and scale tools operate directly on selected objects.

How do Bits work?

Blueprint generation costs 1 Bit and textured Tripo generation costs 6 Bits. New sessions begin with 25 starter Bits.

Does it work on mobile?

The layout adapts to smaller screens and touch input, although a larger screen remains better for detailed 3D editing.

Is Bloxy affiliated with Roblox?

No. Bloxy is an independent creator tool and is not endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
