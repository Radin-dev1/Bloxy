FAQ

Before you hit build.

Does Bloxy publish to my Roblox account?

No. Bloxy prepares a reviewable build and pairs with Studio. You choose when to apply and publish.

Where do Gemini and Tripo keys go?

In encrypted backend secrets—never GitHub Pages, Roblox source, or an .env.example file.

What works right now?

The public workspace is an interactive product preview. Live paid generation remains gated until the backend is securely deployed.

Is Bloxy affiliated with Roblox?

No. Bloxy is an independent creator tool.
